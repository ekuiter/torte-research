### History

Here, we briefly summarize this project and notable developments within its scope.

**May 2018**
We released [kmax-vm](https://github.com/ekuiter/kmax-vm), an [early predecessor](https://github.com/ekuiter/torte?tab=readme-ov-file#history) of torte that automates feature-model extraction with KClause in a virtual machine.

**September 2021**
We released the [feature-model-repository-pipeline](https://github.com/ekuiter/feature-model-repository-pipeline), which extends [kmax-vm](https://github.com/ekuiter/kmax-vm) with an integration of the KConfigReader extractor.

**March 2022**
We released [tseitin-or-not-tseitin](https://github.com/ekuiter/tseitin-or-not-tseitin), which extends the [feature-model-repository-pipeline](https://github.com/ekuiter/feature-model-repository-pipeline) with several CNF transformations and Docker support.

**March 2023**
We released [torte](https://github.com/ekuiter/torte), a consequent evolution of [tseitin-or-not-tseitin](https://github.com/ekuiter/tseitin-or-not-tseitin) that supports generalizing over different experiments and integrates many different tools for feature-model analysis.

**February 2026**
We released this website to summarize our knowledge and insights on the tool ecosystem for feature-model analysis.
